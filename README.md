The rapid expansion of data availability has facilitated the emergence of data-driven, personalized, and dynamic solutions across diverse industries. In the insurance sector, machine learning has been increasingly adopted for pricing to improve risk estimation and enhance insurers’ profitability. However, in the absence of regulation, such approaches may exacerbate adverse selection and lead to the exclusion of high‑risk patients from insurance coverage. Moreover, the reliance on granular personal data raises significant concerns regarding data privacy and security.} To address this, we introduce FL-IMDR (Federated Learning framework for Insurance Market modeling with a Dynamic Regulator). The FL-IMDR is a dynamic regulator framework along with the insurance market modeling as a three-level optimization problem.  We further enable it with differentially private federated averaging to stabilize learning across insurers without centralizing proprietary data. In the numerical 120-month multi-agent simulation in a small market, we demonstrate FL-IMDR achieves improvements across stakeholders with Insured population rising from 60\% to 98\%, increased quality of offered plans and insurer profitability approximately doubles, while trading-off only 9\% increase in mean accepted premium prices. \rvis{In addition,} we show competition shifts from exclusionary cherry-picking to subsidized, high-volume segmentation.

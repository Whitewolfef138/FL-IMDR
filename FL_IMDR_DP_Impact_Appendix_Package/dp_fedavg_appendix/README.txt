Files for the paper appendix
============================

1. Copy appendix_dp_fedavg.tex into the paper after Conclusions and before the bibliography.
2. Copy the PDF figures into the paper's Figures directory:
   - dp_accuracy.pdf
   - dp_convergence.pdf
   - dp_fairness.pdf
3. The executed notebook is FL_IMDR_DP_Impact_Appendix_executed.ipynb.
4. CSV files contain raw, per-seed, aggregated, and paired-test results.

The experiment is a controlled risk-learning ablation based on the paper/repository patient dynamics and DP-FedAvg clipping/noise structure. It isolates the DP effect from price and coverage networks.

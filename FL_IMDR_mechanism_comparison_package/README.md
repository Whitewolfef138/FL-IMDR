# FL-IMDR mechanism-comparison appendix experiment

This package is a **controlled counterfactual experiment** for the manuscript subsection
"Relation to Welfare and Bayesian Mechanisms". It is designed to show that FL-IMDR has a
different decision-authority structure from (i) centralized social-welfare optimization
and (ii) a report-based Bayesian direct mechanism.

## What this experiment does

All mechanisms receive the **same exogenous patient trajectories for each random seed**.
The default experiment uses the manuscript's main market scale:

- 100 patients
- 4 insurers
- 120 monthly rounds
- 50 paired random seeds
- 5-round lock-in period
- target insured share = 0.90
- tax/voucher-pool scaling = 0.10

The package compares:

1. **Central welfare planner**
   - directly chooses plan coverage, premium, patient assignment, and subsidy
   - constrained by actuarial cost and a minimum insurer margin
   - represents the broad decision authority of Eq. (classical welfare), not a claim that
     the particular numerical surrogate is the globally optimal implementation of every
     possible welfare objective

2. **Bayesian direct mechanism**
   - uses reported patient type `(budget, required coverage)` and a known empirical prior
   - applies an allocation rule and transfer rule
   - produces mechanism-induced assignments
   - intended as a transparent, reproducible direct-mechanism counterfactual; it is not
     claimed to reproduce a specific paper's optimal multidimensional Bayesian mechanism

3. **FL-IMDR restricted regulator**
   - insurer prices/coverages are generated by autonomous insurer response rules
   - patients choose their best feasible response
   - regulator changes only vouchers (and an abstract regulatory score parameter)
   - the experiment therefore preserves the structural restriction emphasized by the paper

## Main mathematical diagnostic

The second figure plots the **endogenous-decision override residual**

    R_t =
      assignment override rate
      + 0.5 * (normalized premium override + coverage override).

The autonomous benchmark consists of the insurers' response-generated offers. Patient
best responses are recomputed under the mechanism's realized offers and transfers.

Thus:

- `R_t = 0` means patient assignments coincide with decentralized best responses **and**
  insurer offers are not directly overwritten by the regulator.
- FL-IMDR is constructed to have (up to numerical precision) zero direct override residual.
- The centralized and direct-mechanism comparators generally have positive residuals
  because they directly determine or induce market variables beyond vouchers.

This diagnostic is deliberately about **decision authority**, not about claiming that
FL-IMDR maximizes more welfare than a planner that is allowed to control the whole market.

## Recommended manuscript interpretation

Use the experiment to support a statement such as:

> Under common stochastic market realizations, the three formulations produce different
> market trajectories, but the central distinction is institutional rather than simply
> numerical. The centralized benchmark attains outcomes by directly replacing market
> decisions, and the Bayesian comparator induces allocations through reported types and
> transfer rules. In contrast, FL-IMDR leaves insurer offers and patient selections as
> endogenous responses and acts through its restricted regulatory variables. This is
> reflected by the near-zero endogenous-decision override residual of FL-IMDR.

Do **not** write that FL-IMDR "outperforms the social-welfare optimum" unless the benchmark
objective and feasible sets truly make that statement mathematically meaningful.

## Reproducibility

The experiment is intentionally lightweight and uses only NumPy, pandas, and Matplotlib.
It does not require GPU training. For the final paper, if you have the exact original
FL-IMDR simulator, the cleanest next step is to replace `FLIMDRRestricted.step()` with an
adapter to your production simulator while keeping the paired-market generator, metrics,
plots, and table code unchanged.
